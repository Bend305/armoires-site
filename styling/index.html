The Idea.

Most styling starts with a shopping cart.

Ours starts with your closet.

You already know the feeling. You own good things — things you picked out, paid for, liked enough to bring home — and you still stand there on a Tuesday with no idea what to do with any of it.

A stylist on armoires can see what's actually hanging in your closet, once you invite them in, and build looks out of it. Nothing to buy. Nothing to return. Just someone with better eyes than yours at 7:43 in the morning.

It's the thing we kept wishing existed while we were building everything else.

How It Works.

Four steps. No shopping.

Step 01.

Find someone.

Browse stylists by specialty and read their work before you commit to anything. Everyone sets their own rate, and it's on their profile.

Step 02.

Send a brief.

The occasion. The date. How you want to feel in it. Anything you've saved for inspiration. Takes a minute.

Step 03.

Vote on the looks.

They pull options from your closet. You heart the ones you like, pass on the ones you don't. Be honest — it's how they learn what you actually wear.

Step 04.

Wear it.

The winning look lands in your app with notes on how to pull it off. Try it on virtually first if you're not sure.

The Money.

You pay after someone says yes.

  • Sending a request costs nothing. A stylist has to accept it first.
  • Stylists set their own prices. You see the number before you send anything.
  • We hold your payment until the look is delivered, then give you 72 hours with it. Happy with it? Approve it and your stylist gets paid that day. Forget to? It releases on its own.
  • If something genuinely goes wrong, we'll refund you. We'd rather do that than argue about it.
  • Card details go straight to Stripe. We never see them, and neither does your stylist.
Questions.

The things everyone asks.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. The entire point is the clothes you already own. If a stylist thinks one piece would unlock five outfits, they'll tell you — but nobody here earns a cut from a retailer, and you're never required to buy a thing.

Can a stylist see my whole closet?

Only after you send them a request and they accept it. That's what opens the door, and you can close it again whenever you like. Nobody is browsing your closet uninvited.

What if I don't like what they send?

Vote it down. The voting isn't a formality — it's how your stylist works out what you'll actually put on your body versus what looks nice on a screen. And once a look is delivered, you can ask for tweaks instead of accepting it.

How long does it take?

Depends on your stylist and what you asked for. Each one sets how many clients they'll take in a week, so the people showing as available are genuinely available — not quietly buried under forty other briefs.

Can I use the same stylist again?

Yes, and it's better when you do. They keep everything from last time — what you loved, what you passed on, what you've worn since.

What's the virtual try-on?

The same try-on that's already in armoires. It puts the outfit on you, so you can see the thing before you wear it out the door.

Can I be a stylist on armoires?

We'd love that. You set up a studio — your tagline, what you're good at, your rate, and how many clients you want a week — and once you're approved you're in the directory. Payouts run through Stripe, so your bank details stay between you and them.
